Good Conscious privacy policy

Last updated September 28, 2026.

Good Conscious stores your categories, tasks, people, gratitudes, and settings on your phone only. There is no account and no cloud. We do not sell data, show ads, or send your journal to a server.

Accessibility

The app uses Android Accessibility only to notice when an app you picked is open, so it can offer a reminder. It does not read the screen, passwords, messages, keystrokes, notifications, or anything inside that app. You turn this on yourself during setup.

Installed apps

The app asks to see the names of apps installed on your phone so you can choose which ones to watch. It does not read the contents of those apps.

Battery

The app asks to be allowed to keep running so the phone does not shut it off to save battery.

What leaves the phone

Nothing you write in the app is sent to us. There is no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

Delete your data

To delete everything, use Settings, Privacy and data, Clear all local data, or uninstall the app.
