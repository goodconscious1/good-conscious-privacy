Good Conscious privacy policy

Updated October 8, 2026.

Good Conscious is a nudge app. You put your own life in it. When you open an app you chose to watch, it can offer something from that life before the scroll goes on.

What stays on the phone

Your categories, tasks, reasons, groups, gratitudes, the mood you pick, the people you add, and your settings stay on the phone. The name, the relationship, and the link for a person you have linked are also saved to your Good Conscious account. So do the answers you write for what you can do for them. So do items other people have already sent you, once the phone has saved them. So do notices that a task you sent was completed or forgotten, and the counts on Observations.

We do not keep a permanent copy of that life in the cloud. Uninstalling the app clears that phone only. It does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Google sign-in

Sign-in is optional for the personal side of the app. It is required before someone can link with you or send you anything.

You sign in with the Google account on the phone. We receive the account id Google gives the app, the name on that account when Google provides one, and the email address on that account. The email is used to tell accounts apart. We do not receive your Google password. We do not see your email inbox, contacts, photos, or files.

Your code is that account id, shown as a QR code. It is not shown until you have read the explanation and the phone has accepted your PIN or screen lock.

Linking

Scanning someone's code saves their account id on your phone, next to the name and relationship you gave them. The phone also saves that name, that relationship, and that account id to your Good Conscious account. It does not save their tasks, notes, or messages.

Signing out does not delete a link and does not make anyone scan again. It marks the phone as not accepting new sends. Signing back in with the same Google account turns that back on.

You can pause a person. New sends from them stop until you turn them back on. Anything already waiting stays, and it stays hidden while they are paused. You can unlink or delete them. That removes the cloud flag and anything still waiting from them, so they cannot send again. It only counts once you are online.

Uninstalling the phone does not remove those links. After you install again and sign in with the same account, the names, the relationships, and the links come back. Tasks and everything else were only on the phone, so they do not come back.

What is sent through Firebase

Firebase, a Google service, is the handoff. It is not a permanent inbox. The connection is encrypted.

When you send a mood, a nudge, a gratitude, a task, or one updated answer for what they can do for you, Firebase holds that one item until their phone saves it. The item has your account id, the name saved for you, the words you sent, a short detail such as a reason or a question number, the kind of item, and the time. Their phone then deletes that cloud copy.

If they already have 100 unopened moods, nudges, gratitudes, and tasks from you, the next one does not send. Completed and forgotten notices are not sent through Firebase. They stay on the sender's phone, up to 50, and clear when that list is opened and left.

Delete the account

You can delete the Good Conscious account in the app. Open Settings, then Privacy and Data, then Delete Good Conscious account. That removes the cloud names, the relationships, the links, anything still waiting, and the account, then signs you out. The next sign-in is a new code. The old code stops working. This does not delete the Google account. An app update does not do this, and neither does uninstalling.

If the app is no longer installed, you can ask here: https://goodconscious1.github.io/good-conscious-delete/. Email goodconsciousapp@gmail.com from the Google account you used. The account and the data saved on it are deleted within 7 days. Tasks that were only on the phone are already gone if the app was removed. A Play subscription is not canceled by this request.

There is no separate way to erase the saved names, relationships, and links while keeping the same account.

Accessibility

Watched-app nudges need Android's accessibility switch. This app uses it to read the package name of the app in front, and to know when that app comes forward or leaves. It does not read the words on the screen, your messages, your photos, or your passwords. It does not read what you type. It does not take the clipboard. It does not press keys or tap the screen for you. It does not use your location.

You choose the watched apps. That list stays on the phone. The app can see the names of installed apps so you can pick which ones to watch. It does not read the contents of those apps.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan someone else's code. The picture is not saved and is not uploaded.

Subscription

After three free days, Good Conscious asks you to continue through Google Play. Google charges the monthly price. We do not receive or store your card number. Canceling is done in the Play Store. The subscription stays on the Google account, not on the phone.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not show ads. We do not use your location. We do not post phone notifications for nudges. We do not share this data with other companies for their own use.
